# Build a Taxi Fleet

A web vertical slice of an idle taxi fleet game. Pick a real city, drag from a car to a waiting rider to send it, buy more cars, open your HQ to hire drivers who keep working on their own, and unlock new districts.

Play: https://chrisli328.github.io/build-a-taxi-fleet/

Video (58 s): https://chrisli328.github.io/build-a-taxi-fleet/video/

Best in landscape. Works with mouse or touch.
